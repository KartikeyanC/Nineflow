Privacy

Your planner stays in your own Google Drive.

NineFlow has no server and no database of its own. It runs in your browser, you sign in with your Google account, and your planner is saved in two places only: on your device and in your own Google Drive. This page explains exactly what that means.

What NineFlow can see in your Google account

  • Your name, email address and profile picture, to show who is signed in.
  • Only the Drive files NineFlow creates: a Daily Planner folder containing planner-data.json (and, if two devices save at the same moment, a planner-data (conflict …).json copy, so nothing is lost). Google's drive.file permission means NineFlow cannot see, open or change any other file in your Drive.
  • Nothing else: not your email, contacts, calendar or other files.

What is stored, and where

  • In your Google Drive: your tasks, habits, settings, work log and focus history, in the files above. Google keeps them under your own account and its terms.
  • On this device: a working copy of the same data, so the planner opens instantly and works offline. Changes made offline upload to your Drive when you reconnect.
  • Also on this device: your name, email and picture, so NineFlow can offer "Continue as …" next time, and Google's sign-in token. The token is kept only until you close the browser and lasts about an hour.
  • Nowhere else. NineFlow doesn't send your planner or account details to us or to anyone else. There is no analytics, advertising or tracking.

Hosting

The NineFlow web page is delivered by a hosting provider. Like any website, the host sees standard request details such as your IP address and browser type when the page loads. Your planner itself never passes through the host: your browser sends it straight to Google.

Deleting your data

  • Settings → Clear all data empties your tasks, habits and work log on this device, and the next save empties them in your Drive copy too. Your settings are kept.
  • Settings → Your account → Sign out saves your latest changes to Drive, then removes the planner from this device.
  • To remove everything: delete the Daily Planner folder in Google Drive (this also removes any conflict copies), and remove NineFlow's access to your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google API data

NineFlow's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
