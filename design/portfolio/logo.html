Brand identity · 2026

A mark that moves forward

The logo for NineFlow, a planner, focus timer and work log for web, desktop and Android.

01 — The mark

Three shapes,
one letter

  1. N for NineFlow

    Read together, the shapes draw an N. Read alone, each one says something about the app.

  2. The ribbon is the flow

    One unbroken band from top left to bottom right: the day moving forward, task by task, with no hard corners.

  3. The play shape is the start

    The dark form on the left points right like a play button: press start, and the focus timer runs.

  4. The dot is you

    A person standing on the flow, a step ahead of it, and the focus point the whole app is built around.

02 — Construction

Built on a few simple lines

A 45° flow, one true circle and shared heights keep the mark calm at every size.

45° flow

The ribbon runs on one diagonal from corner to corner.

One circle

The dot is a true circle, about a quarter of the mark's width.

Shared heights

Both dark forms start on the same line, so the N stands level.
