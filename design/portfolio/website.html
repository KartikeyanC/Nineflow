FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I need an account?

No. Choose "Try it without an account" and leave sample data on to see every screen filled in. Your planner stays in your browser.

Where is my planner saved?

Sign in with Google and it's saved to your own Google Drive, or use an email account. As a guest, it stays on your device.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Everything works without a connection, and changes sync when you're back online.

Can I use it on my phone and my laptop?

Yes. The same planner runs on the web, Windows, Mac and Android.

Does it work with my calendar?

NineFlow can keep Google Calendar in step with your plan, and import tasks from a spreadsheet, Todoist, Trello or GitHub.
