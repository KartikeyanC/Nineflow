02 — Principles

Designed for the next five minutes, and the next five weeks

One look at today

The load bar compares planned hours with the working day, so an over-full day shows before it starts.

Add in plain words

"Call the bank tomorrow 3pm !high" fills in the day, time and priority. Speak tasks, or paste messy notes.

Nothing gets lost

Works offline, carries unfinished work forward, and keeps a history of every change on each task.

03 — Views

The same tasks, seen many ways

Every view reads the same tasks, so a change in one shows in all of them at once.

04 — Features

Small features that save the day

Plan the day

A plan that bends, not breaks

Planned hours against the working day, with the finish time. Too full? One tap moves a lower-priority task to tomorrow. Unfinished work from earlier days waits in Carried over, not in a red overdue list.

Interruptions · Ctrl+I

Something came up?

Log urgent work in seconds. It goes onto today's plan, pauses what you were doing, and warns when the day is now over capacity, so the day's report stays honest.

Plan a goal

An exam in 24 days? Every evening, planned.

Name the goal, pick the date, list the topics and the days you're free. NineFlow spreads the work, adds revision at the end and keeps a free day before the deadline. Nothing is added until you confirm.

Add tasks fast

Type it, say it, or press Ctrl+K

"Edit trailer tomorrow 2pm 90m #work !high" turns into chips as you type. Speak a run of tasks, saying "next" between them and "delete that" to take one back. Or paste meeting notes and let AI pick out the tasks.

Focus mode
Focus & finish

Start it, stay on it, finish it properly

A full-screen Pomodoro timer for one task logs the real minutes. Checklists can lock required steps, so a task can't be marked done until they are ticked.

Time & money

From hours worked to an invoice

Tag tasks with a client and mark them billable. Charts show billable hours by client, and one click makes an invoice with the rate, tax and total, ready to print, save as a PDF or export as CSV.

Task panel

Every detail, and every change

Start day, end day, time, length, steps, reminders and a full change history: what changed, from what, to what, and when.

Safe & smart

Nothing lost, and answers on hand

A copy of the whole planner every hour, kept for 30 days, with Undo on every delete. Up to six separate workspaces. And Ask NineFlow answers questions about your own planner in plain words.

05 — Themes

Make it yours

Five colour themes, each in light and dark. The theme recolours sections and buttons; the logo stays orange.

06 — Dark mode

Easy on the eyes after dark

Warm greys instead of pure black, and the same orange, so the brand looks the same day and night.
