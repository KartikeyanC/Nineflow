UI/UX case study · Android app

Made for one hand

The real NineFlow Android app, built with Capacitor from the same React code as the web app. Every screen here is a capture of the running app.

01 — Navigation

Four tabs and a big +

The bottom bar holds the four views people open most. Everything else is one tap away under More, and the orange + adds a task from any screen.

02 — Screens

Everything from desktop, sized for a phone

Home

Today's load, in your thumb's reach

The load bar shows planned hours against the working day and the finish time. When the day is too full, one tap moves a lower-priority task to tomorrow. Work carried over from earlier days waits just below.

Week & task

Swipe the week, tap a task

The day strip switches days with a swipe and the red line marks now. A task opens as a sheet with its status, days, timer and steps, and a big Done button at the bottom.

Add & plan

Plain words in, a plan out

Type "tomorrow 2pm 90m #work !high" and the chips fill themselves, or hold the mic and speak. Plan a goal turns an exam date and a list of topics into an evening-by-evening plan.

Focus & finish

A timer you can hold

The focus timer fills the screen with one task and one big button. Required checklist steps must be ticked before a task is done, with "Finish anyway" still there when it matters.

Revise

Flashcards on the bus

Write question :: answer in any note. Revise shows today's cards one at a time and brings each back after 1, 3, 7, 21, 45 and 90 days.
