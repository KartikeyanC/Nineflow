01 — OVERVIEW

One calm place for the day: plan it, do it, see it.

Most planners stop at a list. NineFlow follows the work from the plan to the finish: what is due today, how long it really took, and what changed on the way. It had to feel the same on a laptop, on a phone and with no internet.

02 — LOGO

The mark

N for NineFlow

Three shapes draw one letter. Read together, they are an N; read alone, each one says something about the app.

The ribbon is the flow

One unbroken band from top left to bottom right: the day moving forward, task by task, with no hard corners.

The play shape is the start

The dark form on the left points right like a play button: press start, the focus timer runs.

The dot is you

A person standing on the flow, ahead of it. The dot is also the focus point the whole app is built around.

03 — CONSTRUCTION

Built on a few simple lines

45° flow

The ribbon runs on one diagonal from corner to corner.

One circle

The dot is a true circle, one third of the mark's width.

Shared heights

Both dark forms start on the same line, so the N stands level.

Clear space

Keep at least the dot's width clear around the mark.

Smallest size

Still reads as an N at 16 px.

07 — UI / UX

Designed for the next five minutes, and the next five weeks.

One look at today

The Dashboard answers three questions first: what's left, how long it takes, and when you'll finish.

Add in plain words

"Call the bank tomorrow 3pm !high" fills in the day, time and priority. You can also speak, or paste notes.

Nothing gets lost

Works offline, carries unfinished work forward, and keeps a history of every change on each task.

08 — DASHBOARD

10 — DETAILS

Small features that save the day

Prompt to list

Messy notes in, a clean task list out

Paste meeting notes or speak a run of tasks. AI picks out each task with its day and priority. Say "delete task 2" or "undo" to fix the list before anything is added.

Task panel

Start day, end day, steps, reminders and a full change history: what changed, from what, to what, and when.

Notes

Sticky notes with pictures, links and #tags. Write question :: answer and it becomes a flashcard.

11 — PLAN THE DAY

A plan that bends, not breaks

Real days don't go to plan. NineFlow shows when a day is too full, keeps yesterday's leftovers in one calm place, and makes room for the urgent thing someone just asked for.

Today's load

Planned hours against the working day, with the finish time. Too full? One tap moves a lower-priority task to tomorrow.

Carried over

Unfinished work from earlier days waits here, not in a red overdue list. Give each one a day, or let it go.

8+

Welcome back. After a week away, people rarely return to a wall of overdue tasks. NineFlow offers a fresh start instead: park the old work in Someday and plan just today.

Interruptions · Ctrl+I

Log urgent work in seconds. It goes onto today's plan, pauses what you were doing, and warns when the day is now over capacity.

12 — PLAN A GOAL

An exam in 24 days? Here is every evening, planned.

1 · Say what and when

Name it, pick the date, list the topics, choose the hours and the days you're free.

2 · Check the plan

Each topic is split over free days, revision comes at the end, and a free day is kept before the exam.

Nothing is added until you press Add 21 tasks.

13 — ADD TASKS FAST

Type it, say it, or press Ctrl+K

Quick add in plain words

"Edit trailer tomorrow 2pm 90m #work !high" becomes chips for the day, time, length, category and priority as you type.

Speak a run of tasks

Say "next" between tasks and "delete that" to take one back. Here "buy milk" is dropped before the list is made.

Command palette

Ctrl+K jumps to any screen, runs an action or finds a task or note.

14 — FOCUS & FINISH

Start it, stay on it, finish it properly

Focus mode
Focus mode

A full-screen Pomodoro timer for one task. Minimise it to a small pill and keep working; the real minutes are logged on the task.

Checklists with required steps

Lock the steps that matter. A task can't be marked done until they are ticked, from the board, the list or the timer. "Finish anyway" is still there when you need it.

15 — TIME & MONEY

From hours worked to an invoice

For freelancers: tag a task with a client, mark it billable, and the timer does the rest.

Hours by client

Billable hours, each client's rate and the amount, for any range.

Ask NineFlow

Ask about your own planner in plain words: "What's overdue?", "How much did I log on Design this week?"

One-click invoice

Every billable task × the client's rate, with tax and a total. Print it, save it as a PDF, or export a CSV.

16 — LEARN & REMEMBER

Notes that turn into flashcards

Write question :: answer on any line of a note. Revise shows today's cards one at a time, then brings each back after 1, 3, 7, 21, 45 and 90 days, while you still remember it.

Revise
17 — ORGANISED & SAFE

Separate planners, and nothing lost

Workspaces

Up to six separate planners under one sign-in: work, study, a side project. Each keeps its own tasks, notes and settings.

Version history

A copy of the whole planner when it opens and every hour while you work, kept for 30 days. See what changed, bring back one deleted task, or restore everything. And every delete has an Undo (Ctrl+Z).
